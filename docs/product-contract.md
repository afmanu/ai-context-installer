# Product contract

## Outcome

Create a local, portable profile that tells compatible AI agents who the user is professionally, what they use AI for and how to collaborate with them safely and effectively.

The installer constructs the profile from six bounded user interactions plus user-approved CVs, documents, websites, social profiles, repositories, instructions and examples.

## Primary user

A knowledge worker, creator, developer, founder, consultant, researcher or learner who repeatedly has to explain their background, preferences, tools, quality bar and approval boundaries to AI assistants.

## Success criteria

A new agent session opened in the installed folder can:

1. describe the user's confirmed professional context and activated use cases;
2. adapt explanations to the user's recorded expertise;
3. follow confirmed working, communication and decision preferences;
4. distinguish allowed actions from actions requiring approval;
5. produce outputs that match the recorded quality bar;
6. apply only relevant conditional specializations;
7. trace important profile claims to approved source metadata;
8. demonstrate 100% applicable schema coverage with no critical conflict.

## Product boundary

The product stores a verified collaboration snapshot. It does not track projects, priorities, tasks, decisions, notes or conversations. It does not import chat history, crawl unprovided sources, connect accounts, modify global agent settings, provide encryption or act as a second brain.

## Initial support

- New dedicated destination folders.
- Claude Code, Claude Co-Work with local access and Codex.
- Files, pasted text and public URLs explicitly supplied by the user.
- Spanish or English profile content.

Existing-workspace adoption, authenticated connectors and additional runtimes require later evidence.
